# Customer data request procedure

Use this procedure when a customer asks to access, correct, export, or delete information held by JOHN Services. Follow applicable law and the organization's approved privacy policy.

## Intake and identity
1. Record the request date, channel, requested action, and a reference number.
2. Verify the requester's identity using an approved proportionate method.
3. Clarify the service, order, or account in scope; do not request unnecessary identity documents.
4. Restrict the request record to staff assigned to handle it.

## Scope and review
- Identify the relevant customer profile, order, messages, attachments, and support records.
- Confirm the requester is authorized to act for the data subject.
- Check whether legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, or dispute obligations require some records to be retained.
- Consult the designated privacy or legal contact when the request is unclear or contested.
- Do not disclose another customer's information or internal security data.

## Fulfilment
- Approve only the minimum information needed for the request.
- Deliver exports through an access-controlled channel and verify the recipient.
- Apply approved corrections consistently to relevant records.
- For deletion, assess legal holds and required retention before removing or anonymizing data.
- Record any refusal or limitation with its reason and reviewer.

## Closure
Record the decision, scope, approver, completion date, delivery method, and unresolved actions. Avoid copying full customer records into the request log.
