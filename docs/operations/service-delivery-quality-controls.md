# Service delivery quality controls

This checklist applies to digital services, application support, document preparation, and customer website work.

## Before accepting work
- Confirm the requested outcome, customer identity, deadline, price, and delivery channel.
- Explain dependencies, third-party fees, and what is outside the agreed scope.
- Never request passwords or one-time codes; use customer-controlled authorization instead.
- Record consent before handling identity documents or other personal information.

## During delivery
- Use a task record with owner, status, due date, and acceptance criteria.
- Keep customer files in approved restricted locations; avoid personal public links.
- Verify names, dates, identifiers, and attachments against customer-provided source material.
- Escalate suspected fraud, altered documents, or requests to misrepresent qualifications.

## Before handover
- Test the delivered website, document, or application workflow against the agreed scope.
- Remove temporary access and test accounts.
- Provide a concise handover, known limitations, and support contact.
- Record customer acceptance and any outstanding actions.

## Service issue handling
Log the issue, impact, first response, resolution, and preventive action. Do not promise outcomes controlled by external institutions.
